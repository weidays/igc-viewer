IGC 查看器：免费在线查看、清理滑翔伞 IGC 飞行记录

光迹 IGC 查看器是免费的在线 IGC 文件查看工具。打开存放飞行记录的文件夹，就能用 ↑ ↓ 键秒切换每次飞行的航迹和高度曲线，自动标出 GPS 跳点和疑似接驳车，上传 XContest 等竞赛平台前，一键清掉废记录。适用于滑翔伞、滑翔机的 .igc 文件，不用安装，不用注册，文件不上传。

也可以直接把文件或文件夹拖进这个窗口。推荐用电脑上的 Edge 或 Chrome。

怎么用

  1. 点「打开文件夹」，选你存 IGC 飞行记录的文件夹，子文件夹也会一起扫描。
  2. 用 ↑ ↓ 键切换，地图上马上显示航迹，下方是高度曲线；鼠标移到曲线上，能看到当时的高度、爬升率和地速。
  3. 点「⚠ 可疑」只看有 GPS 跳点或疑似接驳车的记录，按 Del 标记要删的文件。
  4. 点「删除已标记」，先移到「_已删除」文件夹（可以恢复），确认后再上传竞赛平台。

功能

  • 批量浏览：整个文件夹的 IGC 飞行记录一次列出，按日期或文件名排序，可按飞行员、日期筛选。
  • 飞行信息：日期、飞行员、机型、起降时间、时长、航迹长度、离起飞点最远距离、最高高度、最大爬升和下沉率。
  • GPS 跳点检测：定位突然漂走的点用橙色圈标出。
  • 疑似接驳车检测：降落后忘了关记录仪、坐车回去的路段用紫色虚线标出。
  • 简体、繁體、English 三种语言；地图可选地形图、卫星图或无底图。

隐私

IGC 文件只在你自己的电脑上读取，不会上传到任何服务器，也不需要注册账号。唯一的网络请求是地图底图：浏览器会向 Esri 请求图块，因此会透露飞行区域的大致位置；介意的话，在地图右上角「底图」里选「无底图」。

常见问题

什么是 IGC 文件？

IGC 是国际航空联合会（FAI）定的飞行记录格式。滑翔伞、滑翔机用的飞行记录仪和手机 App（如 XCTrack、Flymaster、SeeYou Navigator）都会生成 .igc 文件，里面按时间记下了位置和高度，XContest 等竞赛平台就是用它来计算成绩。

怎么打开 IGC 文件？

在电脑上用 Edge 或 Chrome 打开本页，点「打开文件夹」选存放 IGC 的文件夹，或者点「打开 IGC 文件」选一个或多个文件，也可以直接把文件拖进窗口。不用安装软件。

上传 XContest 前，怎么筛掉有问题的飞行记录？

打开文件夹后，查看器会在后台逐个检查文件，自动标出 GPS 跳点和疑似接驳车。点「⚠ 可疑」只看这些记录，确认后按 Del 标记，再一键移到「_已删除」文件夹。剩下的记录就可以放心上传了。

我的飞行记录会被上传吗？

不会。文件只在你的浏览器里读取，不会发送到光迹或任何其他服务器。地图底图会向 Esri 请求图块，因此会透露飞行区域的大致位置，介意的话可以选「无底图」。

支持哪些浏览器？

推荐电脑上的 Edge 或 Chrome（Windows、macOS、Linux 都可以），打开整个文件夹和删除文件都需要它们。Firefox 和 Safari 可以打开单个 IGC 文件查看航迹，但不能删除文件。

手机能用吗？

手机浏览器不能打开整个文件夹，只能选单个 IGC 文件查看航迹，不能批量清理。建议在电脑上使用。

Free online IGC viewer for paragliding flight logs

Lumitrail IGC Viewer is a free online IGC file viewer. Open the folder with your flight logs and flip through every track and altitude profile with the arrow keys. It flags GPS glitches and shuttle rides automatically, so you can clean out bad files before uploading to XContest or another competition server. Works with paragliding and gliding .igc files — no install, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

You can also drag files or a folder into this window. Best in Edge or Chrome on a computer.

How to use

  1. Click “Open folder” and pick the folder with your IGC flight logs. Subfolders are included.
  2. Press ↑ ↓ to switch flights. The track appears on the map with the altitude profile below; hover the profile to see altitude, climb rate and ground speed.
  3. Click “⚠ Suspicious” to show only flights with GPS glitches or shuttle rides, and press Del to mark the ones to remove.
  4. Click “Delete marked” to move them into a “_deleted” folder (recoverable), then upload the rest.

Features

  • Browse a whole folder of IGC files at once, sort by date or name, filter by pilot or date.
  • Flight details: date, pilot, glider, takeoff and landing time, duration, track length, max distance from takeoff, max altitude, best climb and sink.
  • GPS glitch detection: points where the position suddenly jumps are circled in orange.
  • Shuttle-ride detection: stretches where you forgot to stop the logger and rode back by car are drawn as purple dashes.
  • English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文; topographic, satellite or no basemap.

Privacy

Your IGC files are read on your own computer and never uploaded to any server; no account needed. The only network requests are map tiles from Esri, which reveal the rough area of your flight. If you mind, choose “No basemap” in the map’s top-right corner.

FAQ

What is an IGC file?

IGC is the flight log format defined by the FAI (World Air Sports Federation). Flight instruments and apps for paragliding and gliding — such as XCTrack, Flymaster or SeeYou Navigator — write .igc files that record position and altitude over time. Competition servers like XContest use them to score flights.

How do I open an IGC file?

Open this page in Edge or Chrome on a computer, click “Open folder” and pick the folder with your IGC files, or click “Open IGC files” to choose one or more files. You can also drag them into the window. Nothing to install.

How do I filter out bad flight logs before uploading to XContest?

After you open a folder, the viewer checks every file in the background and flags GPS glitches and suspected shuttle rides. Click “⚠ Suspicious” to see only those, press Del to mark the bad ones, then move them to a “_deleted” folder in one click. Upload what’s left.

Are my flight logs uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read inside your browser and never sent to Lumitrail or any other server. Map tiles are requested from Esri, which reveals the rough area of your flight; choose “No basemap” if you mind.

Which browsers are supported?

Edge or Chrome on a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux) is recommended — opening whole folders and deleting files need them. Firefox and Safari can open single IGC files to view tracks, but cannot delete files.

Does it work on a phone?

Mobile browsers can’t open whole folders, so on a phone you can only view single IGC files, not clean up in bulk. Use a computer for that.
